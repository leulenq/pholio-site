# Pholio legal architecture and launch review

Prepared September 15, 2026. This is a legal/product working assessment, not a legal opinion or evidence of production compliance. Public documents are rewritten in the existing page shell. No deployment is authorized by this review.

## Decision

The existing corpus is insufficient and materially stale. Preserve its eight routes, rewrite them, and add four purpose-specific documents. Separate the contractual rules from privacy transparency and from actual permission. A published notice cannot establish that the described control has been deployed or that a vendor contract exists.

The launch frame supported by the latest app and strategic record is adult talent, a United States launch focused on New York, free application preparation and submissions, free agency intake, optional Studio+ tools, and event casting distinct from representation. Minors remain an intended later program, not current eligibility. International reference agencies do not themselves establish international account availability. September semantic-search design supersedes the August recommendation to remove search ordering; it does not supersede privacy, discrimination or employment-law requirements.

## Public document architecture

| Route | Function and incorporation |
|---|---|
| `/terms` | Core contract; definitions, account eligibility, publication/content license, service scope, suspension, liability and disputes. Incorporates conduct, submission, subscription and organization terms only as applicable. Privacy notices are acknowledged, not agreed to as optional-processing consent. |
| `/privacy` | Platform-controller notice, collection/source/purpose/audience/retention map, user rights, public-site visitors, optional integrations, minors and international scope. Distinguishes Recipient-controlled data. |
| `/cookies` | Actual shared-cookie and local-storage inventory; functioning `/cookies#preferences` control retained. |
| `/ai-notice` | Optional image analysis, writers, captions/embeddings and search ordering, limitations, consent/deletion and provider roles. Identity biometrics separated. |
| `/community-guidelines` | Enforceable content, anti-exploitation and safe-contact rules; reports and review. |
| `/legal/submission-program` | Direct representation, event/third-party designer disclosure, off-Pholio preparation/self-logging, neutral quotas, snapshots, withdrawal and differing retention. |
| `/dmca` | Copyright notice/counter-notice and repeat-infringer procedure. Agent registration cannot be presumed. |
| `/take-it-down` | Prominent, unauthenticated intimate-image report instructions independent of copyright; statutory notice elements and 48-hour obligation. |
| `/legal/subscriptions` | Studio+ recurring charges, trial, cancellation, refunds and changes; no paid access/selection advantage. Checkout must implement these terms. |
| `/legal/agency-terms` | Organization authority, Recipient purposes, labor/safety, privacy/exports/links and security. Public organization terms are not a substitute for an executed processor agreement and event schedules. |
| `/legal/verification` | Optional Stripe Identity document/selfie flow and private adult context, limited checks/retention, separate biometric consent. |
| `/legal/guardians` | Clearly conditional future program and existing minor-data rights. No invitation to bypass adults-only launch; account authority, named-recipient permission, teen privacy and work permissions remain distinct. |

A standalone universal GDPR DPA is not automatically appropriate. Pholio is a controller for talent accounts, its own discovery, safety, billing and product operation. Recipients independently control application decisions and exported copies. Where Pholio hosts agency-private notes/team records solely on documented instructions, a tailored processor/service-provider agreement with processing and security schedules is needed before relying on that relationship. Do not label all providers “subprocessors” or all agency data “processor data.” See the contextual/operational register for the concrete contract deliverable.

A state-rights section is included in Privacy; do not claim CCPA threshold coverage, an EU representative, transfer certification or universal GDPR compliance without facts. A health-data notice or biometric policy is conditional on actual regulated processing; identity/selfie verification requires a specific biometric assessment even though portfolio AI is designed not to identify faces. No public assurance of nonexistent certification, insurer, staffed inbox or registered agent.

## Identity and version publication

`lessons.md` §6 records that Pholio was not incorporated. `lib/legal-constants.ts` nevertheless claimed a Delaware corporation. Use the brand “Pholio” in the candidate corpus; exact operator identity and service address must be inserted from formation/service evidence before publication. No invented Delaware forum or compulsory arbitration mechanism is carried forward. Mandatory consumer/labor/privacy rights remain unaffected.

Last Updated records the actual revision date: September 15, 2026. Effective Date remains expressly pending because no publication/launch date or live-user transition has been confirmed. These are completed review drafts, not retrospectively operative agreements. A coordinated candidate version is prepared in both repositories; neither should be deployed independently or used to collect acceptance while publication is pending.

First publication: set the actual publication/effective date after operator facts and operational gates are resolved. The revision date can precede publication; it may coincide only if factually true. Terms become binding on each new user through affirmative acceptance. Privacy notices describe practices from their effective date; notice acknowledgment does not legalize historical processing or substitute for consent.

Later substantive revision: preserve the prior text and actual effective dates; prepare and date the new text; select a prospective operative date and adequate notice/consent transition; publish a plain-language change summary; update both corpus versions and all applicable independent disclosure versions; retain exact text/hash shown at acceptance. Do not simply change both displayed dates, overwrite old assent, or record acceptance to text never shown.

The source snapshot at `archive/2026-07-18/` is captured from the working tree before edits, with SHA-256 hashes. It is not proof of what a historical deployment served. Obtain deployment snapshots and acceptance evidence for any historical dispute. The present rewrite must not silently migrate historical consents.
