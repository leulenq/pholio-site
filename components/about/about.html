We only

  1. guess.say what we know.

    A photograph with no date is never called current. A height nobody measured is marked self-reported. An outcome we never saw is never recorded.

  2. sell visibility.charge for what you keep.

    No agency pays us. Nobody pays to apply, to be reviewed, or to be seen. The only thing for sale is craft you keep.

  3. use whatever we are given.share what you agreed to share.

    Location data is stripped from every photograph. An adult’s age is never printed on their card. Permission is asked one purpose at a time, and starts at no.

  4. rank people.let people judge people.

    No scores, no rankings, no guesses at anyone’s potential. Software can tell a close-up from a full length. Who gets signed is a person’s call.

  5. do everything.build the part before the decision.

    Bookings, contracts and commissions belong to the people in the room and the systems they already use. We stop at the door, on purpose.
