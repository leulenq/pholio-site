Pholio exists to make the first look an honest one.

The way into modeling runs on hope.

hope

  1. Rules nobody writes down, so the same photographs get taken again and again.
  2. Silence, which many agencies publish as policy.
  3. Money spent on being noticed: fees to stay visible, paid opinions.
  4. Agencies that turn out not to exist.

Pholio exists to make the way in honest.

Every introduction has two sides.

If you are asking to be seen, you should be able to expect

  • to know the rules before you send anything
  • not to be left waiting
  • never to pay for a chance
  • that your face is not used without asking
  • that nobody promises you work

If you are deciding, you should be able to expect

  • to receive what you asked for
  • not to owe everyone a letter
  • never to pay to receive one
  • that nobody can apply in your name
  • that the decision stays yours

How the company decides

We only guess. sell visibility. use whatever we are given. rank people. do everything. say what we know. charge for what you keep. share what you agreed to share. let people judge people. build the part before the decision.

  1. A photograph with no date is never called current. A height nobody measured is marked self-reported. An outcome we never saw is never recorded.
  2. No agency pays us. Nobody pays to apply, to be reviewed, or to be seen. The only thing for sale is craft you keep.
  3. Location data is stripped from every photograph. An adult’s age is never printed on their card. Permission is asked one purpose at a time, and starts at no.
  4. No scores, no rankings, no guesses at anyone’s potential. Software can tell a close-up from a full length. Who gets signed is a person’s call.
  5. Bookings, contracts and commissions belong to the people in the room and the systems they already use. We stop at the door, on purpose.
