Getting it right

The five things most likely to be written wrong about us, marked up the way your desk would.

  1. Pholio is a modeling agency ‸platform.

    It represents no one, books no work and takes no commission. Representation is an agency’s job, and stays with agencies.

  2. Pholio has launched ‸is preparing to launch.

    When it opens, the date will be at the top of this page.

  3. Its software picks models ‸sorts photographs.

    With permission, and off by default, it can tell a close-up from a full length. It never scores a face or a body. People decide who is signed.

  4. Models pay to be seen ‸apply free.

    Agencies never pay either. Studio+, at $9.99 a month, adds design tools and analytics. Nothing an agency sees or receives changes with it.

  5. Spelled Folio PHOLIO ‸Pholio.

    One word, capital P, said like folio. The capitals belong to the logo.

14.09.2026 Agencies can request access to Pholio, free

Remote, 14 September 2026. Modeling agencies can now request access to Pholio ahead of its launch. Agencies use Pholio to publish what they ask applicants for, receive applications that already meet it, and export them to the tools they already use.

Every request is checked by a person against public records before an account is opened. Pholio does not charge agencies, and has no plan to.

“An open call is usually a form and an inbox,” said Natan Getahun, co-founder. “Both sides deserve better than that, and neither should have to pay for it.”

Requests: pholio.studio/agency/request-access

###

26.08.2026 Statement: what Pholio’s software does with a photograph

Remote, 26 August 2026. Pholio’s image features are off by default and are switched on by the talent, one purpose at a time. When they are on, they describe a photograph: a close-up, a full length, a profile. They do not score a face or a body, guess a person’s age or background, or rank one person against another.

The licence talent give Pholio does not cover training general-purpose AI models on their photographs. The full account is published as the AI notice.
