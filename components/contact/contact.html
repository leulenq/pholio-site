What Pholio is

Is it really us?

  1. Pholio never asks you to pay to apply, or to be seen.
  2. Pholio never offers work, castings or representation.
  3. Pholio never asks for your password.
  4. Pholio staff never ask for photographs by message.
  5. Pholio only writes from addresses ending @pholio.studio

If someone did any of this, it wasn’t us. Don’t pay and don’t send anything. Report it, and we will check.

No office to visit. No phone line. Every address here reaches one of the people who make Pholio, usually within two working days.
