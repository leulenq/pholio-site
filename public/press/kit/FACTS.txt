PHOLIO · FACT SHEET
Revised 4 October 2026

NAME            Pholio. One word, capital P, said like "folio".
WHAT IT IS      A platform for models and the agencies they apply to.
                Talent prepare, send and track their applications;
                agencies receive them.
WHAT IT IS NOT  A modeling agency, a marketplace or a casting service.
                It represents no one and takes no commission.
STAGE           Preparing to launch.
BEGAN           2025
PEOPLE          Two co-founders and an advisor.
WHERE           Remote. No office and no city.
WHO PAYS        Talent, optionally, for Studio+: $9.99 a month or
                $95.88 a year. Agencies never pay.
AGE             18 and over at launch.
AI              Off by default and opt-in, one purpose at a time.
                Describes the photograph, never the face.
FUNDING         None to announce.
WEB             pholio.studio
PRESS           press@pholio.studio
