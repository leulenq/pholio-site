# Site mechanics

Site-only reference. The marketing surface's construction techniques and
register map. The persuasion architecture (why these work on a skeptical
audience) lives in `persuasion.md`; the shared spine in `foundation.md`.

---

## 1. The registers the site refuses

1. **The scam register**: outcomes, flattery, urgency, dream vocabulary,
   fees relocated into packages.
2. **The generic SaaS register**: exclamation, "seamless/effortless",
   discount strips, member counts, self-awarded "most trusted", feature
   grids. To this audience, slop reads as scam-adjacent: a page that looks
   generated looks untrustworthy.
3. **The coach/seminar register**: "unlock your potential", inspirational
   cheerleading, the Barbizon voice. Warmth lives in information, never in
   encouragement.

## 2. The registers the site draws from

1. **The legitimate agency register**: short second-person imperatives,
   physical specifics, rationed warmth, deflation as credibility
   ("phone photos taken by a friend are perfectly acceptable"), and the
   negative-promise block ("we will never..."), which is the industry's
   native trust surface.
2. **The peer-template register**: exact materials, exact specs, real
   numbers, dated. The highest-trust genre with aspirants. Requirement
   pages and guides live here.
3. **Editorial restraint**: the industry's front doors carry almost no
   text; captions name person, client, season. Noun-heavy, verb-quiet, one
   sentence where SaaS uses five, no explained references, no emotional
   superlatives. Renaming the category noun is the Aesop move: the book,
   the card, editions, digitals.

## 3. Sentence toolkit

- **Mechanism, then outcome.** "Resized, re-encoded, and ordered to the
  agency's own spec, so nothing fails silently." Spec earns the emotion;
  emotion never floats alone.
- **Perceptive verb plus receipt** (intelligence without "AI"). Pholio is
  the subject of a perceptive verb (reads, sees, places, checks,
  composes) grounded by one concrete receipt. Numeric humility: report
  the measurement, let the reader own the meaning. Never "we know you".
- **The definite article as positioning.** "The comp card, done properly."
  Sparingly; must be defensible.
- **The corrective assertion.** Negate a belief, replace it: "Talent isn't
  the hard part. Access is." Loud; one per surface at most.
- **The negative promise.** "No fee to submit. No paid visibility." And
  the scoped confession: "Pholio cannot recall copies an agency already
  downloaded." Blemish last, small, correlated with a strength.
- **The dated fact.** "Checked August 19, 2026." "Their own page is the
  authority if the two disagree." Ceding authority outranks any adjective.
- **The one-sentence philosophy.** A brand surface gets one sentence, not
  a manifesto.
- **Epigrammatic fragments** are a marketing-only allowance (contract D3),
  display surfaces only, and every fragment must read as a complete
  thought.

## 4. Page architecture

- **One page, one job**; one primary action; everything else is
  orientation.
- **The artifact argues.** The comp card, the requirement table, the
  receipt do the persuading; a sentence carrying a feature means the
  surface is undesigned (lessons.md §11.1). A cinematic section gets one
  line of prose at most; write the section's vocabulary first, the
  sentence last.
- **Owner-codified display rules** (settled; do not relitigate): at most
  one support sentence of ~20 words under a headline; exactly one
  gold-italic verdict word per headline, never two; one CTA label
  sitewide (`Apply free`); zero em-dashes in visible copy; no eyebrows or
  kickers naming sections; mono voice only as a label on real data.
- **Awareness-stage placement** (persuasion.md §3): requirement pages for
  the problem-aware; comparison-of-approaches for the solution-aware;
  nothing on the site assumes a most-aware visitor.
- **Sitewide furniture carries names, destinations, and facts only**
  (lessons.md §4). Copy that argues belongs on a page read once in
  context.
- Metadata (titles, descriptions) is copy: same rules, plus searcher
  diction from the mined-language corpus.

## 5. CTA and action conventions

- The settled sitewide CTA is `Apply free`. Any additional action label is
  an owner decision, flagged, never silently added.
- Labels state the consequence, not the dream: what the next screen
  actually is, with the cost disclosure within one sentence's reach.
- Never "Join", "Unlock", "Get started", "Learn more" as a crutch, never
  "apply now".
- The only temporal frame is the reader's own readiness ("when your book
  is ready"); never a deadline, cohort, or countdown.

## 6. Site register map

| Surface | Register | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Home and audience heroes | Editorial restraint | Fragments allowed; one claim; the artifact does the arguing; the 20-word support-line cap |
| Requirement pages, guides, scam education | Peer-template | Dense, specific, dated, sourced; glosses on; "what they don't say" sections; the inoculation surface |
| About / brand surfaces | One-sentence philosophy | Guide-not-hero grammar; no manifesto; no transformation sentences with Pholio as subject |
| Studio+ / pricing surfaces | Plain commercial | The one place selling is allowed; craft-and-property claims only; "Nothing an agency sees or receives changes with it" is the model sentence; ROSCA-clean billing language |
| Agency-facing pages (request access, partner surfaces) | Operational institutional | Dense, unglossed, promise-free; the never-block; exit-ramp honesty |
| Parent/guardian surfaces | Process language | Who sees what, who pays whom, what is deleted when; zero flattery of the child |
| Footer, nav, metadata | Names, destinations, facts | No statements, no slogans |
| Legal corpus | Versioned contract language | Never edited for style; a sentence change is a compliance event with the three-step version bump |

## 7. Finish rules

- Zero exclamation marks; vary sentence length; one triplet or corrective
  assertion per page maximum.
- Every visible string read once, cold, alone. Anything an LLM would write
  to sound thoughtful is cut. Boring and correct beats clever and hollow.
- Every number real, sourced, precise rather than round, and wired to
  data that keeps it true. Every "checked on" date carries a maintenance
  obligation.
- The aspiration ceiling (contract D8): before shipping any promise-
  flavored line, ask what the product screen that cashes it looks like.
  If no screen cashes it, the line does not ship.
